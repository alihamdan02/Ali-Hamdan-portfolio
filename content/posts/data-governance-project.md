Designed and implemented a scalable enterprise data classification and governance platform aligned with NDMO/NDI standards, integrating AI-assisted automation for data discovery, profiling, classification, and metadata management.

### The Technical Challenges
Managing, classifying, and governing sensitive data manually across more than 10 heterogeneous enterprise systems containing over 6 million records is highly error-prone. Standard rule-based systems struggle with unstructured attributes, requiring intelligent, automated data profiling and machine-learning-assisted classification workflows.

### Solution & Engineering Highlights
* **Automated Discovery & Profiling:** Processed and analyzed data across 10+ enterprise systems containing more than 6 million records, developing automated data profiling and column-level analysis workflows.
* **AI-Assisted Classification:** Implemented automated and AI-enhanced data classification mechanisms to identify sensitive and regulated attributes, achieving approximately **92% classification accuracy** through automated workflows and expert reviews.
* **Operational Efficiency:** Automated data classification and governance activities, reducing manual effort by approximately **70%** while establishing a reusable metadata management framework and data quality rules.

### Tech Stack
* **Governance & Compliance:** Data Governance, Data Classification, Metadata Management, NDMO, NDI
* **Tools & Platforms:** Informatica IDMC, Data Profiling, Data Quality, Data Validation
* **AI & Databases:** Oracle, Python, AI Data Classification, Metadata Analysis
