Designed and implemented scalable enterprise data integration and data warehousing solutions for the Kidana digital transformation project, supporting high-volume operational and AI-ready data requirements across Hajj and Umrah services.

### The Technical Challenges
Integrating heterogeneous enterprise systems like SAP with high-volume transactional data required robust pipelines that could handle massive loads without degrading performance, while ensuring data quality, historical tracking (SCD), and preparing clean structured datasets suitable for advanced analytics and AI consumption.

### Solution & Engineering Highlights
* **Enterprise Integration:** Integrated data from SAP and multiple enterprise source systems using Informatica IDMC and Cloud Data Integration (CDI), covering ingestion, staging, transformation, validation, and warehouse loading.
* **Optimized Pipelines:** Developed batch and incremental data pipelines to efficiently process large datasets and newly created or modified records while minimizing unnecessary full data loads.
* **Data Governance & Modeling:** Implemented Slowly Changing Dimensions (SCD), Data Profiling (DF), and Data Quality (DQ) processes alongside fact and dimension data models to deliver trusted, clean datasets for Power BI Analytics and downstream AI workflows.

### Tech Stack
* **ETL & Integration:** Informatica IDMC, CDI, ETL / ELT, Incremental Loading
* **Data Management:** SCD, Data Profiling, Data Quality, Data Warehouse, Dimensional Modeling
* **Databases & BI:** SQL Server, SQL, Power BI, SAP
