Designed an enterprise-grade on-premise AI platform for government data intelligence, enabling secure AI capabilities to operate within controlled infrastructure while maintaining strict data privacy requirements.

### Key Implementation Details
* **On-Premise Deployment:** Deployed open-source models (such as `gpt-oss-20b`) locally using **Ollama**, ensuring no sensitive data left the government infrastructure.
* **Knowledge-Aware Retrieval:** Implemented a robust **Graph RAG** and vector search system, enabling the AI to reason over complex relationships between tables, columns, and business entities.
* **Automated Governance:** Engineered specialized multi-agent systems to analyze database schemas across 10 enterprise systems (over 6 million records), automatically identifying sensitive data and proposing data quality rules.

### Project Impact
* Achieved **92% classification accuracy** for sensitive metadata detection.
* Reduced manual data classification and compliance auditing effort by approximately **70%**.
